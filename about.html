Our Story

Founded in 2024, PEKKA was born from a passion for combat sports and a desire to create the highest quality gear for fighters of all levels. We believe that the right equipment can make all the difference, and we are dedicated to providing our customers with the tools they need to succeed. Our journey started in a small garage, with a handful of prototypes and a big dream. Today, we are proud to be a leading name in the industry, trusted by professional athletes and aspiring champions around the world.
